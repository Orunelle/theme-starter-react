# Metropolitan

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web. Standalone React/TanStack Start Orunelle theme, separate from Furniture.

## Users and purpose
Fashion shoppers discover collections, inspect garments and variants, manage their bag, and use Orunelle customer and checkout flows. Merchants customize editable home sections, navigation, imagery, text and colors.

## Scope
User explicitly requested a complete, clean clone-like theme of https://metropolitan-main.myshopify.com/. The reference pins composition and visual language: full-height fashion photography, minimal overlay navigation, side panels, unboxed product grids, editorial collections, lookbook and stories, restrained product detail and footer. Provide English and Arabic.

## Product truth
Live catalog, stock, cart, customer authentication, checkout and orders remain authoritative in the Orunelle SDK/API. Reference product data appears only in explicitly identified local demo mode; live merchants see their own catalog. A demo bag may persist locally for interaction previews; demo checkout cannot place orders. No Shopify scripts, paid theme source, real merchant data, credentials, warranties or reference-store contact details are copied.

## Constraints
128 source files, 512 KiB per source file, 2 MiB total managed source. Bundle key editorial images and fonts efficiently; configurable reference product images are external in the local demo. Publication follows normal independent release review. No current merchant storefront is replaced by creating this theme.
